# Release notes

## MOSS 1.0 — A SILT Story

First release.

- Native Game Boy ROM, `MOSS_by_Synth_Centric.gb`: 256 KiB, MBC5 + RAM +
  battery, 8 KiB save, DMG only (no Color features).
- A five-day story: arrival, listening, rain, the last afternoon, departure.
  Eight places: Mae's cottage, the village, Whispering Wood, the creek, the old
  railway, the hill, the hidden clearing and the pond.
- Seven people and creatures: Mae, Iris, Mr Hollis, Ada, Mr Vale, the cat and
  the reed creature, with dialogue that changes across the days.
- The Sound Notebook: eight discoveries with illustrations, a daily hint page
  and a last page after the ending.
- Six listening puzzles: the Hidden Path, the Three Stones, the Rain Shelter,
  the Last Bell, the Still Clearing, the Pond's Answer.
- The pond instrument: SILT's 8 × 5 garden and stones A and B; Pebble, Bell,
  Reed, Root and Wood tools; four scales; four densities; tempo; Wind (Drift);
  rain; echo; water; insects; hold; a just-listen mode.
- A generative composer on the four DMG channels with phrase memory,
  call-and-response insects, slow harmony, weather and controlled silence.
- Musical memory that learns intervals, rhythms, voices, scale and density,
  saved with the game and resettable.
- An ending composed from the player's own pond; post-game Return to the Pond
  with eight presets and a personal pond slot.
- Two-copy checksummed saves with recovery.
- Album package: covers (3000 and 1000 px, text-free, back), SVG sources,
  promotional banner, social graphic, cartridge label mock-up, ten-track
  companion soundtrack recorded from the ROM, manual (Markdown and PDF), liner
  notes, the boy's notebook.

See testing/KNOWN_LIMITATIONS.md before distributing.
