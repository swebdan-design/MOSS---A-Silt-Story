# Credits

**MOSS — A SILT Story**
An interactive musical adventure for the original Game Boy.

**Synth Centric** — concept, the SILT world, artistic direction.
Dan Schwebel, known as Synth Centric, is an Australian beat maker, music
producer, writer and educator.

**Development, writing, pixel art, generative music system, artwork,
documentation and testing** — produced with Claude (Anthropic) working as the
development team on Synth Centric's brief, building on the SILT trilogy's
source and documentation.

**SILT lineage** — SILT (Nintendo DS), SILT 2 — The Living Pond and SILT III —
The Last Visit (Nintendo 3DS), by Synth Centric. MOSS reuses SILT's original
5 × 7 pixel alphabet (MIT) and adapts its pond rules: the 8 × 5 garden, stones
A and B, the 48/64 and 32/44 ripple cycles, D minor and C major pentatonic at
76 BPM, Calm/Drift, rain densities and the no-feedback echo.

## Tools

GBDK-2020 4.3.0 and SDCC 4.4.1 (compiler and runtime) · Python 3 · PyBoy 2.9.0
and SameBoy 1.0.1 (testing and soundtrack capture; SameBoy's own open-source
boot ROM) · RGBDS 0.9.1 (to build SameBoy's boot ROMs) · Pillow, NumPy,
ReportLab · FFmpeg (FLAC/MP3 encoding) · DejaVu fonts (manual).

No Nintendo BIOS, firmware or proprietary assets are included or used.
